- A request that has waited 5 s says why on its process's stderr (the CLI's for a client, the daemon's log for a pool worker; a daemon a client starts appends its stderr to `daemon.log` in its data directory, set aside as `daemon.log.old` once past 4 MiB, checked at start and every minute while it runs): no free slot, or the bytes it needs against those charged and the cap, and how to raise that cap.
  It repeats every minute or when the reason changes, and says when it is admitted.
  A request larger than the whole disk cap says so and waits until the cap is raised.

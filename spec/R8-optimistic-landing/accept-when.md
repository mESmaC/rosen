- **Accept when:** a clean after-land change lands with neither receipt nor approval in under 200 ms; a three-change before-land stack with approvals on every layer and one receipt on the top lands as three commits in one request in under 200 ms, and a child's checked receipt survives a separate parent land without a new check.
  The base only ever moves by a land whose merge is clean (and, in `before-land` mode, whose receipt and approval match), or by a server revert; a receipt is reused when its checked tree matches exactly, or under the configured reach/merge re-check rule above; and a crash at any point leaves the base at either the old or the new commit.
  A land with 20 clean edit overlaps leaves all 20 heads unchanged, and the next land does not wait for their probes.
